REID ATTORNEYS INCORPORATED

Founded by Rustum Keith Reid in 2010, Reid Inc is a leading boutique law firm, based in Upper Wynberg, Cape Town providing specialist conveyancing, litigation and advisory legal services.

The practice offers experienced and skilled attention to all its clients and is now established as a full complement law firm able to advise and litigate on a wide spectrum of matters. Our experience spans the fields of civil, criminal, commercial and immigration law in all forums including the Magistrate Court, High Court, Labour Court and Supreme Court.

Our approach is based on the understanding that in the current dynamic environment, we must put the needs of our clients first and find ways to best service their particular needs in the most professional and cost-effective manner.

Most of our Clients have been with us for over a decade because we believe that positive relationships are the foundation of our business. In these uncertain times we offer our clients reliability, excellence and affordability.
