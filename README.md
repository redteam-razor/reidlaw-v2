# Reid Attorneys & Reid Realty Website

A modern, professional website for Reid Attorneys and their property division, Reid Realty.

## 🌐 Live Site

**Main Site:** https://reidlaw-v2.vercel.app  
**GitHub:** https://github.com/redteam-razor/reidlaw-v2

## ✨ Features

### Reid Attorneys (Main Site)
- **Professional Design:** Modern, clean layout with professional blue/orange color scheme
- **Hero Section:** Full-screen hero with background image and call-to-action buttons
- **About Section:** Complete firm history with Abraham Lincoln quote and law office imagery
- **Services Display:** 5 service cards (Labour Law removed as requested):
  - Dispute Resolution
  - Corporate & Commercial Law
  - Criminal Law
  - Estate Administration & Planning
  - Property Law
- **Legal Query Form:** Complete form with:
  - Full name
  - Email address
  - Phone number
  - Query type dropdown (5 service options)
  - Message textarea
  - Form validation and success message
- **Smooth Navigation:** Sticky header with smooth scrolling

### Reid Realty Page
- **Dedicated Property Page:** Separate landing page for Reid Realty division
- **6 Property Services:**
  - Residential Sales & Purchases
  - Commercial Property
  - Property Management
  - Conveyancing Services
  - Property Investment Consulting
  - Property Valuations
- **Property24 Integration:** Prominent call-to-action linking to https://www.property24.co.za/
- **Why Choose Us:** 4 key differentiators highlighting legal expertise
- **Cross-Linking:** Easy navigation between law and property divisions
